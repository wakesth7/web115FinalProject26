# Story Overview
This story adds a seventh character, Budd Lynn, a local gas attendant, few miles off of the Blackwood cabin. His grand father used to own the property back in the day. . His own life however, has not been kind to him lately as he's been knee-high on his gambling debts. 

As the group of six close friends knew about the upcoming winter storm, they went to the gas-station where Budd was working to purchase last minute supplies before the storm. Budd could not help but notice the well-to-do friends and their expensive jeweleries. He learnt quickly that they were headed to the Blackwood cabins.Growing up, he oftern used to visit the cabins with his grandpa so he knew the ins and outs of the cabins. He now knew there would be valueables at the cabins. He decides to make a move later that night. All he had to do now was to wait! 

# Actions taken
He drives his old delivery truck during the heat of the storm knowing the blackout will have knocked the power out at the cabins. Having overhead one of the friends share the safe passcode while at the store, all he needed was to find a safe passage. He knew the cellar window was the weak spot. He enters through the window as such, inputs the passcode in the safe and makes away with cash and several other valueables.
